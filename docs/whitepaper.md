# Flint Whitepaper

> **秉火前行。** — *Carry the fire forward.*

> Status: **v1**. 白皮书是一种发声,也是之后那篇 paper 的背景——
> 不是技术规格(规格在 README 与 paper)。
> *A statement of intent, and the background for the paper to come — not a spec.*

## 道 · Why

最早的火不是胜利,是守夜——微弱、会灭,却让人暂时不把命运交出去。
*The first fire was never triumph. It was a night watch — fragile, perishable, just enough to keep fate from being handed over.*

`HIGH TECH, LOW AGENCY HUMAN`

这是 PocketPunk 给 **punk** 的定义,也是 AI 时代里我们几乎共同的去向:技术越来越强,
能自己做主的越来越少。读、写、记、判断、决定,一件件被替你做完;等你回神,事情还在
发生,只是不再由你。
*That is how PocketPunk defines* punk *— and where almost all of us are heading in the
age of AI: the technology around you grows stronger, the room to decide for yourself
grows smaller. Reading, writing, remembering, judging, choosing — done for you, one by
one. By the time you look up, things are still happening, just no longer by you.*

Flint 不许诺把这些扳回来。它只守一件小事——**让"你还是你"这件事,不至于在你不知情时,
被悄悄抹掉。**
*Flint does not promise to win any of it back. It keeps one small thing: that "you are
still you" is not quietly erased without your knowing.*

## 它守的 · What it keeps

它能学会你,但不能擅自改写你。
它做的每一件会留下的事,你都看得见、能反悔、带得走,不锁在谁的服务器里。
什么进入你的长期记忆,由你点头,不由它。
有些底线不靠跟它讲道理来守,靠物理来守——再聪明,也烧不穿。

*It can learn you, but it cannot rewrite you.*
*Everything it does that lasts, you can see, undo, and carry away — locked in no one's server.*
*What enters your long-term memory, you nod to; it does not.*
*Some lines are not held by reasoning with it, but by physics — however clever, it cannot burn through.*

它不替你判断对错,也不保证你不犯错。它只保证:**你犯的错是你自己的,你改的,也是你
自己的。** 一次点头,证明的是"我允许",不是"我英明"。
*It does not judge right from wrong for you, nor keep you from being wrong. It keeps only
this: the mistakes you make are yours; so are the fixes. A nod proves* I allowed it, *not*
I was right.

## 秉火前行 · Carry the fire forward

火不属于谁。第一缕是闪电给的,你只是举着它走一程的人。
所以这从来不是占有,是接力——在答案出现之前,守住,带走,交到下一只手里。而每一次交接,
仍由人,亲手。
*Fire belongs to no one. The first was struck by lightning; you only carry it a stretch.
So this was never ownership — it is a relay: while the answer has not come, keep it, carry
it, hand it to the next pair of hands. And every handoff is still made by a human, by hand.*
